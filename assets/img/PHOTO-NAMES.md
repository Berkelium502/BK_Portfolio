# Photos go in this folder

Name each photo exactly as below (lowercase). JPG is best; .jpeg and .png also work.
Any photo that's missing just shows as a grey box with its file name on the site.

## Home page
| File name | Where it shows | Shape |
|---|---|---|
| `portrait.jpg` | Photo of you at the top, next to the intro | Tall (4:5) |

## Tips
- iPhone photos in **HEIC** format won't show in browsers. Export them as JPG first
  (on iPhone: Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible).
- Keep each photo under about 1 MB (around 2000 px wide) so the site loads fast.
- Before posting work photos from United or Cummins, check they're allowed to be shared.
