# Background video for the top banner

Save your clip here as **`banner.mp4`** and it plays automatically, silently, and on a loop behind
your name at the top of the home page. A dark overlay keeps the text readable.

Tips:
- MP4 (H.264) works in every browser. Other formats may not.
- Keep it short (5–15 seconds) and small (under ~8 MB). Send it to Claude and it can trim and compress it for you.
- There's no sound, so pick something with motion: the car running, machining, a layup, etc.
