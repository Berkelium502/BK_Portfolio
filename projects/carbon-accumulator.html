Overview

In-house manufacturing of the carbon fiber accumulator structures for Illini Electric Motorsports' FSAE EV.

What I did

  • Co-led the accumulator structures project with a teammate
  • Designed manufacturing jigs and developed ply schedules for the carbon fiber layups
  • Machined the mold and built the manufacturing timeline, working around tooling and schedule constraints to manufacture in house
  • When the team lost access to on-campus manufacturing, reworked the process to manufacture the full set of accumulator structures in about two weeks
  • Finished ahead of the previous year's schedule, cutting 2 months from production
  • Designed jigs for inverter manufacturing and accumulator case welding
  • Designed, manufactured, and tested an IP68 low-voltage enclosure
